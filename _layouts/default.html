Bryant Shaoheng Fang

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at UT Austin, advised by Qixing Huang and supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the UT College of Natural Sciences Dean's Prestigious Fellowship. I also work closely with Georgios Pavlakos. My research interests include world models, video generation, and agent systems for visual generation.

I am currently a research intern at Roblox. Previously, I interned at Adobe Research and DAMO Academy, Alibaba, and was a visiting researcher at UCL. Before that, I was a master's student in the Department of Computer Science at UT Austin, advised by Qixing Huang and Raymond Mooney. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with Siheng Chen.
